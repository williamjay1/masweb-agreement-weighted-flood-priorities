# Annual RMA exposure rankings

This retrospective comparison ranks counties by actual reported annual RMA Summary of Business acreage or liability. The source records group corn and soybean underwriting by commodity year, state, county, commodity and insurance plan. Four archived queries cover Minnesota and South Dakota in 2023, Iowa in 2024 and Missouri in 2025, using plans 01, 02 and 03. The 1,663 returned rows equal the total counts reported by their complete query responses, are unique across the grouping keys, and report acreage in Acres. The annual reporting measures do not use indemnity values or the manuscript's month- and cause-matched loss records to build the ranking. They nevertheless reflect annual records from the same commodity years and relevant insured populations, and their availability before each event has not been established. We refer to reported acreage and liability for these three plans rather than total physical cropland or exposures across all plans.

We use the original 95% support candidate counties in the four events with positive total indemnity. Wisconsin remains outside the four-event utility calculation because its original outcome total is nonpositive. We select counties using the original 20% county equivalent budget with fractional allocation at the cutoff and split any cutoff ties. Lift is the share of the frozen event indemnity captured divided by 0.20. The equal-event mean is calculated over the four original events. We validated the original AWC selections against the frozen selection weights before reusing that score. The underlying AWC scores are held fixed when the candidate set changes.

Thirty of 34 counties have annual reported exposure for the selected plans. The four without a matching annual record are Missouri FIPS 29067, 29153, 29209 and 29213, all with zero matched event indemnity. In the fixed-set scenario, the county denominators and 20% budget remain fixed. The four unreported counties sort below counties with positive reported exposure for the two annual rankings. An absent record is distinct from a confirmed zero, and the county output preserves missing numeric amounts. In the reported-counties sensitivity, the four absent counties are excluded for all three rankings, and the budget is recalculated on the reduced county set. Missouri's budget changes from 2.8 to 2.0 county equivalents. Annual reported acreage and liability yield identical selections in both scenarios. The resulting mean lifts are exploratory comparisons in these events.

Install `pandas` and `numpy`, then run `python annual_exposure_comparison.py` from this directory within the supplement. Use `--supplement PATH` if the script is stored separately. It uses frozen input scores and the archived RMA provider snapshots already included in the supplement. All hashes and request scopes appear in `annual_exposure_manifest.json`; detailed candidate rows and weights and all four event estimates are provided in the accompanying CSV files.
