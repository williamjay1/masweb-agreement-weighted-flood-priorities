# Sources and reuse

The authors' code, analyses, derived tables and original graphics are available under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The rights and access terms of records obtained from other providers continue to apply.

- **USGS.** Map services and data available from U.S. Geological Survey, National Geospatial Program. The five frozen WBD geometries come from layer 6 of the [WBD Map Service](https://hydro.nationalmap.gov/arcgis/rest/services/wbd/MapServer/6). The five originals are compressed without alteration in the supplied ZIP. The Missouri stream stage snapshot is from USGS National Water Information System. [National Map terms](https://www.usgs.gov/faqs/what-are-terms-uselicensing-map-services-and-data-national-map)
- **USDA RMA.** The official 2023 Cause of Loss ZIP and four archived Summary of Business responses originate from the U.S. Department of Agriculture Risk Management Agency. They supply the loss dates and unit/exposure fields for the archived calculations. [Cause of Loss data](https://www.rma.usda.gov/tools-reports/summary-of-business/cause-loss) · [Catalog entry and public domain notice](https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/cause-of-loss-historical-files)
- **NOAA/NWS and Iowa Environmental Mesonet.** Local storm reports come from the National Weather Service, as served through Iowa State University's Iowa Environmental Mesonet. VIIRS derived comparisons are accompanied by the original NOAA product identifiers. [NWS disclaimer](https://www.weather.gov/disclaimer) · [Mesonet terms](https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/disclaimer.php)
- **Satellite providers.** OPERA DSWx-HLS and eligibility products are identified in the acquisition and source manifests. Copernicus Emergency Management Service GFM, Sentinel-1, JAXA ALOS-2, NASA SWOT PIXC and NOAA VIIRS satellite assets are distributed by their respective providers. The archived tables and scripts describe the observations, checks, scene IDs and applicable coverage. [GFM product and access](https://orbit.eodc.eu/dataset/GFM) · [NASA/JPL SWOT PIXC product](https://podaac.jpl.nasa.gov/dataset/SWOT_L2_HR_PIXC_D)

## Obtaining original inputs for selected checks

- The original semigeneralized county geometry has SHA-256 8ecc920ee3d9366dc13d6f6e566463b6249f017e913aedd0a5bd00fc6ddd4d43. For exact replication, obtain data/ancillary/census_counties_2025/counties_2025_semigeneral.geojson from the version 1.0.5 archive and place the file in r2/inputs/. The vendor entry is [AlderMaps US Boundaries Semi-General v2](https://www.arcgis.com/home/item.html?id=a48f9224e29d487ba104244e9d81c643). The official 2025 Census TIGER file is a separate input for the boundary sensitivity; see the analysis README for its source.
- The published comparisons based on GFM source TIFFs use the official catalog and product links in new_review_analyses/sar_comparison/ and new_review_analyses/sar_followup/. EXTERNAL_SOURCE_CHECKSUMS.csv records the expected original checksums and paths. The live catalog and product revisions may change.
- For the 30 May 2025 Missouri VIIRS comparison, use the GLB019 NetCDF and GeoTIFF named in viirs_formula_20261124/producer_formula_comparison.json. Supply their paths with --netcdf and --geotiff. [NOAA JPSS product distribution](https://noaa-jpss.s3.amazonaws.com/index.html)
- For the Missouri SWOT check, use the eight Version D PIXC files listed in missouri_swot_pixc_20261115/SWOT_PIXC_granule_manifest.csv, with the archived NASA byte lengths and MD5 values. The derived 5,661-point optical sample and nominal footprint membership are included.
- Some historical screening, optical preprocessing and map-rendering scripts use external inputs or plotting assets. The relative external/ paths identify required locations to be supplied locally. Refer to the adjacent provenance and result files before attempting those runs. This release supports selected analyses from the supplied inputs and does not reconstruct full scenes from original imagery.
