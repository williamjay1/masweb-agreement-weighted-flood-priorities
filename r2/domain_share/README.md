# Retained support in each frozen event domain

This comparison uses the five primary events in the 2026-11-24 review revision. The numerator is the summed common valid eligible 30 m pixels inside accepted 500 m cells, multiplied by 0.0009 km² per pixel. Accepted cells have at least one eligible pixel, at least 95% common valid eligible coverage, and positive common valid pixel counts. The denominator is the polygonal area of the frozen geometric domain before applying agricultural and water eligibility masks. The resulting share quantifies the observed agricultural support relative to the full geometric domain. It differs in denominator and numerator from the extracted candidate cell retention and eligible pixel retention already reported in Table A2.

The optional nominal whole-cell area multiplies the number of accepted cells by 0.25 km². Whole cells can include ineligible, invalid, or outside-domain portions. The nominal fraction is a comparison to the reviewer's suggested cell-count arithmetic and does not measure observed agricultural area.

The copied original `noaa_blind_analysis_domain_registry.csv`, `noaa_blind_analysis_domains.gpkg` and `noaa_blind_analysis_domain_freeze_report.json` are from `revision_geocarto_v4/results`. The original domain freeze completed on 2026-08-03 at 18:15:11 UTC and preserves six preflight events, including the Michigan sensitivity pair. `revision_geocarto_v4/scripts/freeze_blind_analysis_domains.py` lines 81–105 project county polygons and both sets of tile footprints to EPSG:5070 and intersect the county union with the common sensor footprint. Lines 124 and 139–143 calculate geometric area and write the EPSG:5070 GeoPackage and registry. The registry's snapped rectangular bounds and dimensions describe the raster grid, so rectangular area is not used as the denominator. `revision_geocarto_v4/scripts/build_blind_event_eligibility.py` lines 398–410 apply crop, persistent-water and event geometry eligibility to the pixels after the geometries were frozen.
