# Missouri SAR follow-up: availability and limited overlap

The original frozen sample has 562 accepted 500 m cells and 5,661 strict common valid eligible 30 m optical pixels. Their S30 and L30 acquisition times were 2025-05-30 16:58:49 and 16:48:07 UTC. The registered event was May 23 to May 27. This is a post hoc availability assessment, not an independent flood accuracy test.

## GFM

At the original optical common pixels, 16 candidate Copernicus GFM scenes within ±7 days were screened in the preceding audit. We expanded to 123 scenes from 2025-05-01 16:48:07 through 2025-06-29 16:48:07 UTC, downloading their GFM observed-water products and screening each pixel with nearest-neighbour reprojection to the fixed original 30 m EPSG:5070 grid. `gfm_60day_candidate_downloads.csv` and `gfm_60day_eligible_pixels.csv` give every scene and common count. Scene bounds are not valid coverage; only values 0 or 1 are treated as valid, 255 is missing.

The nearest nonempty GFM observation is the 2025-05-26 00:20:30 UTC GFM v4.0.0 scene `ENSEMBLE_FLOOD_20250526T002030_VV_NA020M_E084N030T3`, approximately 112.46 to 112.64 hours before the two optical products. It covers only 197 of 5,661 original optical common pixels (3.48%), in 27 of 562 accepted cells. On this very small common domain, GFM reports zero water pixels and S30 and L30 report 20 and 22 respectively. `compare_may26.py` reconstructs and verifies the frozen cell-level optical denominators and counts, then generates `missouri_may26_confusion.csv` and `missouri_may26_manifest.json`. The point of this analysis is to document insufficient coverage and the time discrepancy, not to estimate or claim classification accuracy. A blank or zero radar-positive count must never be reported as a validated absence of flooding in the full domain.

The nearer in time GFM catalog records of May 29 and May 31 have zero valid pixels in this fixed domain. The closest much more complete candidate is May 21 00:12:35 UTC, whose largest tile covers 5,633 pixels and reports six observed water pixels. It is approximately 232.59 hours before L30 and predates the registered event, so it is not an event-window validation. June 14 provides a similar large footprint roughly 343 hours after L30. GFM uses historical water references and ancillary datasets; the comparison is not ground truth. No additional GFM advisory flag filter was applied.

## Alternative datasets and access

The Microsoft Planetary Computer public `sentinel-1-rtc` collection was queried over the full accepted-cell bounding rectangle from 2025-05-23 through 2025-06-06. Only two intersecting scenes were returned, both from Sentinel-1A on May 26 at 00:20 to 00:21 UTC. For each exact optical pixel centre, `screen_rtc_points.py` transforms to the RTC grid and samples source VV and VH intensity cells. Scene 1 had zero jointly valid samples, scene 2 had 229 valid VV and VH samples across 29 accepted cells, including 20 S30-water and 22 L30-water centres. This is 4.05% of the original 5,661 centres. The 10 m RTC direct-centre sampling and the categorical GFM nearest-neighbour reprojection are different sampling operations, so their denominators must not be treated as identical. RTC is a backscatter intensity, not a flood mask; we did not choose a threshold or assert classification accuracy. No RTC layover/shadow quality mask was independently applied. `rtc_may26_point_samples.csv` contains sampled source intensities, not ground truth.

NASA CMR search of official collection `C2949811996-POCLOUD` over the same region and May 23 through June 6 returned five DSWx-S1 granules, all dated May 26 around 00:20 to 00:21 UTC. An anonymous HTTPS request for the B02 product returned HTTP 401 and redirected to Earthdata Login. Neither successful download nor an actual DSWx-S1 pixel comparison is claimed. Metadata snapshot is in the original evidence folder.

The RAPID archive bucket is publicly listable without a signature, but searches of the exact prefixes `RAPID_Archive_Flood_Maps/2025`, `.../20250526/` and `.../20250531/` returned `KeyCount=0` and `IsTruncated=false` in this access check. The AWS registry's general date range alone does not establish an available May 2025 scene. This does not establish that no RAPID data exist elsewhere. Source: https://registry.opendata.aws/rapid-nrt-flood-maps/ and https://github.com/QingYang6/RAPID-NRT-flood-maps-on-AWS/blob/master/README.md.
