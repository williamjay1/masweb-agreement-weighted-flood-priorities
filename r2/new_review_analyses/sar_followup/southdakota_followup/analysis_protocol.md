# Prospective South Dakota SAR dark-backscatter diagnostic

Protocol saved before inspecting pixel values or comparison results. The source is ESA Sentinel-1A IW GRD radar, processed to terrain-corrected linear gamma0 by Microsoft Planetary Computer RTC. Search all scenes intersecting the fixed accepted optical domain from April 9 to 11, 2023 and use the two April 10 slices of the same orbit. Compare to the original accepted 500 m cells, applying the original eligibility and joint optical validity rules.

Resample VV linear power to the exact 30 m EPSG:5070 optical grid by area-weighted average with source nodata excluded; same-orbit scene overlaps are averaged in linear power. A common pixel requires both optical products and the SAR source to be valid, finite and positive. Use 10 log10(power). Primary diagnostic marks VV below -17 dB as dark backscatter, based on a published Sentinel-1 water mapping threshold from Huang, Nguyen, Zhang, Cao and Wagner (2017), https://www.mdpi.com/2220-9964/6/5/140; sensitivity cutoffs -19 and -15 dB are fixed before observing any outcomes. No rule is calibrated with optical products. Report full 2 x 2 cross-classifications, Jaccard, valid coverage and positive prevalence for each threshold and optical sensor.

This is an exploratory dark-backscatter comparison, not established flood extent, not an independent truth set, and not an accuracy assessment. The cited study used sigma0, adjusted the -17 dB threshold against a water reference, and used a terrain mask in its own study setting; it noted significant false positives with a single threshold. Thresholds here are applied to RTC gamma0 without a terrain mask and may not transfer. The entire diagnostic is a post hoc exploration, although cutoffs were specified before inspecting these pixel values. Snow/ice, dry soil, vegetation, terrain shadows, wind and speckle affect values. We do not use HAND, local incidence, separate quality flags, baseline change detection or field observations; no claim of a validated water algorithm, cause, or product superiority is warranted. The threshold sensitivity and actual comparison outcomes must be reported together.
